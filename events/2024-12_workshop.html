Winter International Workshop on Social Stratification and Mobility

Published

December 6, 2024

Social Stratification and Mobility workshop organized by the SSM2025 research group, The University of Tokyo.

Venue: Ito International Research Center (B1 Gallery 1, 2), The University of Tokyo

Friday, December 6

12:30-13:00 Registration

13:00 Welcome (Sho Fujihara and Kikuko Nagayoshi)

Introduction of two speakers (Fumiya Uchikoshi)

13:15 Plenary session

Ohjae Gowen, “Deinstitutionalizing Overwork and Women in the Workplace”

13:55 Discussion (Ryota Mugiyama)

14:05 Reply/Q&A

Ohjae Gowen is an Assistant Professor of Sociology (ABD) at Singapore Management University. His research focuses on work-family dynamics and their implications for gender inequality and demographic behavior. He received his Ph.D. in Sociology from Harvard University in 2024.

Ryota Mugiyama is an Associate Professor in the Department of Political Studies at Gakushuin University. His research focuses on labor market inequality, intergenerational mobility, and family formation. He received his Doctorate in Sociology from the University of Tokyo in 2019.

14:15 Leafia Ye, “Immigrants’ Earnings Mobility and Later-Life Health”

14:55 Discussion (Tate Kihara)

15:05 Reply/Q&A

Leafia Ye is an Assistant Professor of Sociology at the University of Toronto. She received her Ph.D. in Sociology from the University of Wisconsin-Madison, and a Bachelor’s degree in Sociology and German from the University of Hong Kong. She is broadly interested in inequalities by immigration status and in the aging process. She’s currently the Principal Investigator of the project “immigrant integration and later-life health,” which is funded by the U.S. National Institutes of Health.

Tate Kihara is an Assistant Professor in the Graduate School of Media and Governance at Keio University. His research examines international migration to and from Japan in both historical and contemporary contexts, with a particular focus on its relationship to social inequality. He earned his Ph.D. in Sociology from Brown University in 2023.
